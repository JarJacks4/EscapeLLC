Frequently asked

What's the difference between an ambassador and an affiliate?

Ambassadors post weekly for 12 months and lead campaigns, with higher rates and bonuses. Affiliates share at their own pace with a flat rate, and can upgrade later.

How are downloads counted?

You get your own link or code. Downloads that come through it count toward your payout.

How do I get paid?

Monthly, by PayPal, Venmo or ACH. The minimum payout is $25; smaller balances roll over to the next month.
